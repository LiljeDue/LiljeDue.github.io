## Working with me as your co-supervisor

Troels is your main supervisor, and his
[homepage](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~athas/) already covers the
things that apply to every project he runs: weekly meetings at a fixed
slot, an agenda or a cancellation sent the evening before, current
code ready to demonstrate, how many hours of supervision the contract
allocates, and how the report and defense work. All of
that holds. This letter only covers what is specific to me, so you
know what to expect from a project with two supervisors rather than
one.

We will discuss everything below at our first meeting, and you should
say if something does not fit how you work.

### Who does what

As mentioned, Troels will be your main supervisor, and how our work is
split depends from project to project and on what week it is.
Sometimes he will provide feedback and sometimes I will. What is most
certain is that I will be easy to get hold of for help with smaller
problems that are not worth waiting for a meeting, such as conceptual
problems with the literature or technical problems. If the project is
one that I came up with, I will likely be a driving force in how
student and supervisors envision the project. That said, it is your
project and you are assessed on your own reasoning, so treat my
suggestions as suggestions rather than instructions, and push back
when you disagree with me.

There is a limit to this, though. The project we set out is one that
Troels and I have judged can actually work as a project, in scope and
in what it has to live up to. So if you want to take it somewhere we
think will not hold up, we will say so, and bigger changes of
direction are settled with Troels.

I take no part in assessing your project. It is assessed by Troels and
a censor, and the learning goals in your project contract are the most
important part of that assessment, so keep coming back to them as you
work. If you are ever in doubt whether what you are doing is enough,
or whether it still meets the goals you wrote down, that is a question
for Troels and not for me. Since I have no say in the grade, nothing
you tell me along the way counts against you, and you can be as open
with me as you like about what is not working.

### Getting hold of me

- **Email**, on my home page, as it is with Troels academic homepage.
- **Discord** (handle given on request).
- **My office**, 772-01-0-S25 at the PLTC Section in the H.C. Ørsted
  Institute. You are welcome to knock.

Discord and my office are for the informal things, by which I mean
anything that stops mattering once it is answered: a build that will
not work, a bug you cannot find, where something lives in the
codebase, whether a result looks plausible, or a paper you cannot make
sense of. Ask about that whenever you like.

Anything that changes the project itself belongs in an email. It will
then be dealt with either over mail or at a meeting where Troels is
there. That means the scope, the problem you
are answering, the learning goals, the plan, or anything you would
want to be able to look up again in two months. Nothing about the
direction of the project gets settled at my door or in a chat window,
because Troels also needs to be part of it.

If you are not sure which kind of thing you have, just ask me, and I
will tell you when something needs to be written up or taken to a
meeting. Sorting that out is not your job.

I usually reply within a day or two. If you have not heard from me in
three working days, assume the mail got buried and write again.
Reminding me is not rude and it is not a bother. It is genuinely the
most useful thing you can do.

### What to send me

You can send me minor questions, code or other problems that do not
need to be resolved at the meeting. For the meeting, the expectations
are the same as for Troels: a written agenda. You should see having me
as a co-supervisor as an opportunity to contribute to my research, and
also to get more frequent input on the problems you are stuck on. So
please send broken programs, conceptual problems, or paragraphs that
are hard to pin down.

### How I will work with you

I am closer to your level than Troels is, and I was stuck on a lot of
the same things fairly recently. I believe this will help you in your
work, in that you will have more of an eye level experience when
working with me. I am likely to be invested in your work, since if you
choose projects I suggest, they are related to my research. The other
thing is that I should be quite easy to get in contact with, so help
with minor problems you are stuck on can easily be sorted out.
