I am willing to be a co-supervisor on Masters and Bachelor projects. Here are
some projects I have in mind and what it is like to have me as a co-supervisor,
where [Troels Henriksen](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~athas/) would be the main
supervisor.

## What sort of projects I want to supervise

My interest is generally within the data-parallel programming domain. I am
interested in how you would map algorithms which do not seem parallel to
data-parallel hardware, like GPUs. This is usually done using Futhark, and the
goal is to have a high-level program which runs quickly or at least solves a
complex problem. Problems I have looked at include parsing, lexing, type
checking, and hash maps.

## Working with me as your co-supervisor

Troels is your main supervisor, and his
[homepage](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~athas/) already covers the things that
apply to every project he runs: weekly meetings at a fixed slot, an agenda or a
cancellation sent the evening before, current code ready to demonstrate, how
many hours of supervision the contract allocates, and how the report and defense
work. All of that holds. This letter only covers what is specific to me, so you
know what to expect from a project with two supervisors rather than one.

We will discuss everything below at our first meeting, and you should say if
something does not fit how you work.

### Who does what

As mentioned, Troels will be your main supervisor, and how our work is split
varies from project to project and depends on what week it is. Sometimes he will
provide feedback and sometimes I will. If the project is one that I came up
with, I will likely be a driving force in how the student and supervisors
envision the project. That said, it is your project and you are assessed on your
own reasoning, so treat my suggestions as suggestions rather than instructions,
and push back when you disagree with me. There is, however, a limit to this: the
project must be approved by Troels and me, and we will only approve the project
if we can see it as an academically viable project.

I take no part in assessing your project. It is assessed by Troels and a censor,
and the learning goals in your project contract are the most important part of
that assessment, so keep coming back to them as you work. If you are ever in
doubt whether what you are doing is enough, or whether it still meets the goals
you wrote down, that is a question for Troels and not for me, since I have no
say in the grade.

### Getting hold of me

- **Email**, on my home page, as it is with Troels' academic homepage.
- **Discord** (handle given on request).

Discord and my office are for the informal things, by which I mean anything that
stops mattering once it is answered: a build that will not work, a bug you
cannot find, where something lives in the codebase, whether a result looks
plausible, or a paper you cannot make sense of. Ask about that whenever you
like. I usually reply within a day or two. If you have not heard from me in
three working days, please write to me again.

Anything that changes the project itself belongs in an email. It will then be
dealt with either over mail or at a meeting where Troels is there. That means
the scope, the problem you are answering, the learning goals, the plan, or
anything you would want to be able to look up again in two months. Nothing about
the direction of the project gets settled at my door or in a chat window,
because Troels also needs to be part of it. If you are not sure which kind of
thing you have, just ask me.

### What to send me

You can send me minor questions, code or other problems that do not need to be
resolved at the meeting. So please send broken programs, conceptual problems, or
paragraphs that are hard to pin down. For the meeting, the expectations are the
same as for Troels: a written agenda.

### How I will work with you

I am closer to your level than Troels is, and I was stuck on a lot of the same
things fairly recently. I believe this will help you in your work, in that you
will have more of an eye-level experience when working with me. I am likely to
be invested in your work, since if you choose projects I suggest, they are
related to my research.
