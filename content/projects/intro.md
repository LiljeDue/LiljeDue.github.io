I am willing to co-supervise Master's and Bachelor's projects, with [Troels
Henriksen](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~athas/) as main supervisor. Below are
the kinds of projects I have in mind and what it is like to have me as a
co-supervisor.

## Projects I want to supervise

I work on data-parallel programming, in particular how to map algorithms that do
not look parallel onto data-parallel hardware such as GPUs. This is usually done
in Futhark, with the goal of a high-level program that runs fast or at least
solves a complex problem. Problems I have worked on include parsing, lexing,
type checking and hash maps.

## Working with me

Troels's [homepage](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~athas/) covers what applies to
all his projects: weekly meetings at a fixed slot, an agenda or cancellation the
evening before, current code ready to demonstrate, the supervision hours in the
contract, and how the report and defense work. All of that holds. This page only
covers what changes when there are two supervisors. We will go through it at
our first meeting, so tell me if something does not fit how you work.

### Who does what

How Troels and I split the work varies by project and by week, so feedback may
come from either of us. If I proposed the project, I will likely help shape its
direction. It is still your project, and you are assessed on your own
reasoning, so treat my suggestions as suggestions and push back when you
disagree. Troels and I must both approve the project, and we only do so if it
is academically viable.

I take no part in assessment. Your project is graded by Troels and a censor,
mainly against the learning goals in your contract, so keep returning to them.
Questions about whether your work is enough, or still meets those goals, go to
Troels.

### Getting hold of me

You can reach me by email (listed on my homepage) or on Discord (handle on
request).

Use Discord or my office for anything that stops mattering once it is
answered: a broken build, a bug you cannot find, where something lives in the
codebase, whether a result looks plausible, or a paper you cannot make sense
of. Broken programs and paragraphs you cannot get right are welcome too. I
usually reply within a day or two. If you have not heard back in three working
days, write again.

Anything that changes the project itself goes by email: the scope, the research
question, the learning goals, the plan, or anything you will want to look up in
two months. It then gets settled by email or at a meeting with Troels present,
never with me alone. If you are unsure which kind of question you have, ask.

### How I will work with you

I am closer to your level than Troels is, and I was stuck on many of the same
things not long ago, so I hope to offer a more eye-level perspective. If you
pick a project I suggested, it will be close to my own research, so I will be
invested in it too.
